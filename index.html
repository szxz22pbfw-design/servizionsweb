The problem

Two fears every product team carries.

01 — The adoption gap
0% of healthcare
technologies fail.

The technology works. The system around it was never designed.

Most healthcare technologies never reach the clinicians and patients they were built for. The device is validated. The clinical pathway around it isn't.

02 — The wrong next product

"The next eighteen months of roadmap rides on this decision."

Even with a successful product, deciding what to build next is guesswork.

Trend reports are generic. Engineering teams need direction. Boards want evidence. The decision sits with the CPO — and the cost of being wrong is real.

How we're different

Engineers zoom in.
We zoom out.

Your engineering team designs the device. Servizions designs the system around it — the clinical workflow, the patient journey, the futures the product has to survive. Both disciplines are required. Most companies only have one. That's the gap we close.

How we work

Three acts. Each one earns the next.

1Act 1 · Clinical Intelligence

We go into the clinical environment you are building for.

Observe sessions. Interview clinicians. Map the moment-by-moment reality your team has never formalized.

Output Clinical Intelligence Brief
2Act 2 · Product System Map

We work with your product and clinical leadership to develop three strategic options.

Grounded in what we observed, not in innovation theory. Each option carries the constraints of the clinic it has to live in.

Output Product System Map + three options
3Act 3 · Strategic Direction

We stress-test the three options against the futures already in motion.

You receive the Scenario Cone — a decision-support tool defensible to your board, your investors, and your engineering team.

Output Scenario Cone + Strategic Roadmap
