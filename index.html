Our Approach

At Servizions, we work at the service system level of healthcare innovation. Healthcare technologies are often developed as products, but healthcare itself is a complex service involving patients, clinicians, workflows, and environments. Our approach focuses on understanding how technology interacts with real clinical practice. By mapping patient journeys, clinical workflows, and service environments, we help companies align innovation with the realities of healthcare delivery. Rather than focusing only on the product, we examine the entire service system in which the technology will operate.

Healthcare technology development is typically driven by engineering, research, and regulatory processes. While these are essential, they often leave little room to deeply explore how technologies will be experienced in everyday clinical settings. As a result, innovations that perform well technically may still face challenges in adoption, workflow integration, and user acceptance. Servizions addresses this gap by connecting technology development with the real-world context of healthcare services.
