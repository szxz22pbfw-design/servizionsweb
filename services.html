01The three acts

From clinical reality to strategic direction.

Each act is a complete engagement with its own deliverable. You can stop after Act 1. You can stop after Act 2. Most clients move through all three, in sequence, because each one builds the trust and evidence the next one requires.

1
Act 1 · Clinical Intelligence

We go into the clinical environment you are building for.

Direct observation of clinical sessions. Interviews with clinicians and patients. Moment-by-moment mapping of the interaction between patient, clinician, and device. Structuring what your team has seen but never formalised — or has never seen at all.

Output Clinical Intelligence Brief
Duration 2–3 weeks
2
Act 2 · Product System Map

We work with your product and clinical leadership to develop three strategic options.

Service blueprint. Friction map. Meso-level workflow — one patient, one therapist, every moment mapped. Friction categorised as product, service, or training. Three strategic options grounded in observed clinical need, not innovation theory.

Output Product System Map + three options
Duration 5–7 weeks
3
Act 3 · Strategic Direction

We stress-test the three options against the futures already in motion.

Four to five healthcare futures built from clinical data. Each option scored against each future. The Scenario Cone — a decision-support tool defensible to your board, your investors, and your engineering team. Clear answer on each option: invest here, wait on this, abandon that.

Output Scenario Cone + Strategic Roadmap
Duration 10–12 weeks
02How they fit

Each act earns the next.

Act 3 is never sold cold. Strategic product direction is the highest-stakes deliverable Servizions produces. Nobody buys it from someone they have never worked with.

Act 1 builds trust by showing things your team missed in the clinic. Act 2 creates urgency by mapping where your product fails to fit. Act 3 is the conversation you ask for, after Acts 1 and 2 have made the case.

You can engage Servizions for Act 1 alone — and many clients do. The Clinical Intelligence Brief is a complete deliverable on its own.

But the work compounds. Each act sharpens the next. By the time you reach Act 3, the futures are not built from desk research — they are built from what we observed in your clinical environment, in your clinicians' workflow, in your patients' day. That is the entire commercial advantage.
