Three acts. Each one earns the next.

Servizions delivers strategic product direction in three structured engagements. Each act is a complete deliverable on its own. Each one earns the right to the next. Below: what each act contains, the deliverable that anchors them, and the five-phase methodology underneath.

01The three acts

From clinical reality to strategic direction.

Each act is a complete engagement with its own deliverable. You can stop after Act 1. You can stop after Act 2. Most clients move through all three because each one builds the trust and evidence the next one requires.

1
Act 1 · Clinical Intelligence

We go into the clinical environment you are building for.

Direct observation of clinical sessions. Interviews with clinicians and patients. Moment-by-moment mapping of the interaction between patient, clinician, and device. We structure what your team has seen but never formalised — or has never seen at all.

What's inside
  • Direct, structured observation in your target clinical environment
  • Semi-structured interviews with clinicians and, where possible, patients
  • Stakeholder mapping across clinical, administrative, and technical actors
  • Workflow context analysis at the micro level (single moments, single contact points)
  • Clinical signal coding into structured findings ready to power Act 2
Output Clinical Intelligence Brief
Duration 2–3 weeks
2
Act 2 · Product System Map

We work with your product and clinical leadership to develop three strategic options.

Service blueprint. Friction map. Meso-level workflow — one patient, one clinician, every moment mapped. Friction categorised as product, service, or training. Three strategic options, co-designed with your team, grounded in observed clinical need — not innovation theory.

What's inside
  • Service blueprint with frontstage and backstage actor mapping
  • Meso-level session workflow — one patient and one clinician, end to end
  • Friction categorisation: product problem, service problem, or training problem
  • Adoption barrier analysis with specific implications per category
  • Co-design of three strategic product options with your product and clinical leads
Output Product System Map + three options
Duration 5–7 weeks
3
Act 3 · Strategic Direction

We stress-test the three options against the futures already in motion.

Four to five healthcare futures built from clinical signals — not trend reports. Each option scored against each future. The Scenario Cone — a decision-support tool defensible to your board, your investors, and your engineering team. Clear answer on each option: invest here, wait on this, abandon that.

What's inside
  • Four to five healthcare futures built directly from observed clinical signals
  • Stress-test matrix — every option scored against every future
  • Build-readiness filter applied (cost, complexity, time-to-market)
  • Build sequence: which option first, which in parallel, which to deprioritise
  • The Scenario Cone deliverable — visual, defensible, ready for the board
Output Scenario Cone + Strategic Roadmap
Duration 10–12 weeks

The deliverable is a cone.

Every other foresight firm draws a scenario cone. Servizions is the only one whose narrow end contains observed clinical reality — not market data. That is the entire differentiator. It makes every option, every scenario, and every recommendation grounded in what actually happens between a patient and a clinician with a device.

Narrow end

Clinical ground truth

What we observed. The meso-level workflow, the friction map, the field research from your specific clinical environment. This is what makes the cone different from any other foresight firm's output. Without this, the cone is built on assumptions.

The cone

Healthcare futures

Four to five credible futures for your specific domain. Not built from trend reports — built from the clinical signals we observed. Each future is already in motion. These are not predictions; they are trajectories.

Wide end

Three strategic options

The product directions you can choose from. Each option is positioned inside the cone based on which futures it survives, which it thrives in, and which ones make it obsolete. Decision-support tool — not a report.
