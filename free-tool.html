How this blueprint works. The process follows the six principles of human-centred design in ISO 9241-210 [8]. The eight areas follow the SEIPS work-system model (people, tasks, tools and technology, physical environment, organisation) [1, 2]. Complexity is rated per area as simple, complicated or complex, following the NASSS framework and its complexity-assessment tools [3, 4]; NASSS proposes that the more domains are complex, the lower the chance of sustained adoption. The adoption questions reflect widely used technology-acceptance factors — usefulness, effort, social influence and supporting conditions [5] — and the field checks draw on reviews of robots in nursing care and hospitals [6, 7]. The scoring itself is a Servizions heuristic: a prompt for discussion, not an expert judgement.
