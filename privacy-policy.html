For this purpose, the browser you are using must connect to Google's servers. As a result, Google becomes aware that this website has been accessed via your IP address.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the uniform display of content).

Third country transfer: Transmission to Google's servers in the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Further information: Google Privacy Policy.

⚠ Legal status notice The Regional Court of Munich I ruled on 20 January 2022 (Case No. 3 O 17493/20) that embedding Google Fonts via CDN without prior user consent can constitute a GDPR violation. We are currently evaluating local embedding (self-hosting) of the fonts used to avoid this transmission to Google. This section will be updated after the transition.

§7Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or web analytics. Only technically necessary cookies are set, insofar as these are required for the operation of the website (e.g. by GitHub Pages or by Formspree for spam protection).

Legal basis: §25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG (technically necessary) or Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

You can set your browser to inform you about the setting of cookies and to allow cookies only in individual cases, to exclude the acceptance of cookies for certain cases or in general, and to enable the automatic deletion of cookies when closing the browser.
