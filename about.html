01The studio

A studio for the work that happens before development.

Most MedTech failures are not technology failures. The device works. The clinical pathway around it was never designed. The service system around the device — the workflow, the patient journey, the futures the product has to survive — is the layer where adoption is won or lost.

Servizions is the studio for that layer. We work upstream of development: before the engineering budget is committed, before the product line is locked, before the next eighteen months of roadmap is decided.

The work happens in the clinic, not the report. That is the entire commercial advantage.

02The method

Systemic innovation, applied to clinical reality.

Three inseparable characteristics that map directly to the three acts of every Servizions engagement.

1
Foundation of Act 1

Systemic Thinking

Understanding interconnectedness. The intersection of systems thinking, critical thinking, and design thinking. Identifying the whole, the parts, and the relationships between them. This is what we do when we go into the clinic — observing and structuring the present scenario.

2
Foundation of Act 2

Systemic Approach

Focusing on the entirety of the system. Strategic synchronisation of components, acknowledging complexity, building synergy. This is what we do when we map the product system and co-design strategic options with your team.

3
Foundation of Act 3

Innovation Network

Collaboration and coordination across stakeholders, disciplines, and resources. Integration of capabilities across the boundary between market and hierarchy. This is what we do when we stress-test options against the futures already arriving.
